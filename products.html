With RC Billing, businesses can streamline their daily workflow, keep their records organized, monitor performance, and deliver a faster, more professional billing experience — all from one portable application.

About Safe uR Fin Device Locker

Safe uR Fin is an advanced, military-grade digital finance locker and mobile security management platform designed specifically for smartphone retailers and finance providers. It protects store investments on financed devices with zero-latency remote device control, preventing loan defaults and protecting asset value.

Tamper-Proof Hardware Security

Factory reset protection, anti-uninstall safeguards, and automated offline SMS lockdown ensure full retailer control even if the customer disables internet or removes the SIM card.

From instant application locking and live GPS breadcrumb trails to automated full-screen EMI overdue buzzers, Safe uR Fin empowers retail stores to finance smartphones with complete confidence.

2. Device Location (Real-Time GPS Tracking)

It automatically fetches and displays the current customer's real-time location in the device location monitoring dashboard area.

Automated Real-Time Location Fetch & Geofencing

The system queries high-accuracy satellite GPS coordinates alongside cellular tower triangulation to instantly plot the device on Google Maps. View live pinpoints, city, postal address, movement history, and receive automatic alerts if the smartphone crosses retailer-configured city geofence boundaries.

3. SIM Information & Hardware Telemetry

It displays the current SIM name, carrier, and phone number currently in use on the customer's phone.

Carrier Intelligence & SIM Change Detection

Live console tracks telecom carrier (e.g., Jio 5G, Airtel 4G, Vi), active phone number, SIM serial (ICCID), and international subscriber ID (IMSI). If a new SIM is inserted or original SIM is detached, the dashboard updates immediately and alerts store admins.
